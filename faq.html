MyPlace is in San José del Cabo, Baja California Sur, Mexico, next to the golf course and close to the historic center and the beaches. The coordinates are 23.04622° N, 109.70324° W, and you can open the exact spot in Google Maps from the cover of our home page.

There are 55 suites and residences. Choose from king hotel rooms, a queen studio, and one- and two-bedroom suites with king and queen beds. Most have a full kitchen, and some units connect to add bedrooms.

Most units do. The suites and the studio have a full kitchen and a living area. The king hotel room has a coffee station and no kitchen.

Pools, a palapa for unwinding in the open air, a 24/7 front desk for arrivals and departures at any hour, security, and parking on the property. A concierge is available to help you plan your stay.

The historic center of San José del Cabo, with galleries, plazas and open-air tables, is close, and on seasonal Thursdays the Art Walk fills the streets. Palmilla and Costa Azul are the nearby beaches, the San José Estuary is good for birdwatching at dawn, and the golf course is steps from the property. Whale season runs from December to April.
